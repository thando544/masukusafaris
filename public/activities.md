# Things to do in Victoria Falls and Chobe

## [Elephant and Cheetah Experience](https://masukusafaris.com/activities/elephant-and-cheetah-experience)

A memorable wildlife encounter featuring close-up experiences with elephants and cheetahs.

- Location: Victoria Falls, Zimbabwe
- Duration: Half Day
- Price: $190

## [Non-Private Safari Game Drive](https://masukusafaris.com/activities/non-private-safari-game-drive)

Join a shared safari drive and explore the beauty of African wildlife.

- Location: Victoria Falls, Zimbabwe
- Duration: 3 Hours
- Price: $80 - $120

## [Chobe Full Day Trip](https://masukusafaris.com/activities/chobe-full-day-trip)

A full-day adventure to Chobe National Park with wildlife viewing and unforgettable scenery.

- Location: Chobe National Park, Botswana
- Duration: Full Day
- Price: $170 - $200

## [Victoria Falls Jet Boat Ride](https://masukusafaris.com/activities/victoria-falls-jet-boat-ride)

An exciting high-speed adventure on the water near Victoria Falls.

- Location: Victoria Falls
- Duration: 1 Hour
- Price: $100 - $130

## [White Water Rafting](https://masukusafaris.com/activities/white-water-rafting)

Take on the rapids for one of the most thrilling adventures in Victoria Falls.

- Location: Victoria Falls
- Duration: Half Day / Full Day
- Price: $160

## [Tour The Victoria Falls (Zimbabwe & Zambia Combined)](https://masukusafaris.com/activities/tour-the-victoria-falls-zimbabwe-zambia-combined)

Explore both sides of the majestic Victoria Falls in one combined experience.

- Location: Victoria Falls / Livingstone
- Duration: Half Day
- Price: $80

## [Sunset Cruise](https://masukusafaris.com/activities/sunset-cruise)

Relax and enjoy breathtaking sunset views on the water.

- Location: Zambezi River
- Duration: 2 Hours
- Price: $50 - $90

## [Night Game Drive and Bush Dinner](https://masukusafaris.com/activities/night-game-drive-and-bush-dinner)

Enjoy a unique night wildlife experience followed by a memorable bush dinner.

- Location: Victoria Falls
- Duration: Evening Experience
- Price: $200

## [Victoria Falls Bungee Jump](https://masukusafaris.com/activities/victoria-falls-bungee-jump)

Leap into one of the most thrilling adventure experiences near Victoria Falls.

- Location: Victoria Falls Bridge
- Duration: Short Activity
- Price: $160 - $180

## [Victoria Falls Quad Biking Adventure](https://masukusafaris.com/activities/victoria-falls-quad-biking-adventure)

Ride through exciting terrain on a fun and energetic quad biking adventure.

- Location: Victoria Falls
- Duration: 1 - 2 Hours
