## Itinerary

### Day 1: Welcome to Victoria Falls

Stay: Ilala Lodge Hotel

- Arrival at Victoria Falls and private transfer to Ilala Lodge.
- Relax in a deluxe room at a boutique lodge bordering the national park.
- Optional tailored activities can be arranged at extra cost.

### Day 2: Explore Victoria Falls

Stay: Ilala Lodge Hotel

- Breakfast at the lodge.
- Explore Victoria Falls, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
- Optional activities include scenic walks, helicopter flights, Devil’s Pool, rafting, cultural excursions, and rhino tracking.

### Day 3: Road Transfer to Chobe National Park

Stay: Chobe Game Lodge

- Shared road transfer across the border into Botswana.
- Check in at Chobe Game Lodge, the only permanent lodge located inside the national park.
- Enjoy included expertly guided safaris in electric vehicles and silent boat cruises.

### Day 4: Safari on the Waterfront and in the National Park

Stay: Chobe Game Lodge

- Experience Chobe through land and water-based safaris.
- See one of Africa’s most famous elephant destinations.
- Enjoy a peaceful and immersive safari experience with all-inclusive hospitality.

### Day 5: Fly from Chobe to the Delta

Stay: Camp Okavango

- Scenic flight from Kasane to the Okavango Delta.
- Short transfer to Camp Okavango on Nxaragha Island.
- Settle into a two-night all-inclusive stay in the heart of the Delta.

### Day 6: Explore the Okavango Delta

Stay: Camp Okavango

- Enjoy motorboat safaris through the reed-lined channels.
- Experience traditional mokoro excursions and guided bush walks on nearby islands.
- Discover exceptional birdlife and the tranquil beauty of the Delta.

### Day 7: Fly to Maun

Stay: End of tour

- Scenic flight back to Maun.
- Airport drop-off for onward travel arrangements.
