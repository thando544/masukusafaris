## Itinerary

### Day 1: Arrival in Nairobi

Stay: Nairobi hotel

- Arrival in Nairobi and meet-and-greet by your professional driver guide.
- Transfer through Nairobi to your hotel.
- Settle in and relax ahead of the safari.
- Suggested accommodation options include Villa Rosa Kempinski, Argyle Grand Hotel, or The Boma Nairobi.

### Day 2: Nairobi to Amboseli National Park

Stay: Amboseli lodge or camp

- Drive from Nairobi to Amboseli National Park, approximately 240 km, taking around 4 to 5 hours.
- Enjoy the first views of Mount Kilimanjaro rising above the plains.
- After lunch, head out for your first game drive in Amboseli.
- Look out for large elephant herds, lions, giraffes, zebras, and wildebeest against the famous Kilimanjaro backdrop.
- Suggested accommodation options include Soroi Amboseli Camp, Sopa Lodge, or AA Lodge Amboseli.

### Day 3: Full Day in Amboseli National Park

Stay: Amboseli lodge or camp

- Spend a full day exploring Amboseli National Park.
- Early morning game drive with sunrise views over Mount Kilimanjaro.
- Look out for elephants, lionesses, cheetahs, hippos, buffalo, and abundant birdlife.
- Enjoy lunch during the day’s exploration before continuing with afternoon wildlife viewing.
- Suggested accommodation options include Soroi Amboseli Camp, Sopa Lodge, or AA Lodge Amboseli.

### Day 4: Amboseli to Lake Nakuru National Park

Stay: Lake Nakuru lodge

- Depart Amboseli and travel to Lake Nakuru National Park, approximately 320 km with a driving time of 6 to 7 hours.
- Descend into the Great Rift Valley and enjoy scenic viewpoints along the way.
- Arrive at Lake Nakuru, famous for flamingos, rhinos, Rothschild giraffes, and beautiful lake scenery.
- Enjoy wildlife viewing and sunset over the lake.
- Suggested accommodation options include The Cliff, Sopa Lodge, or Sarova Woodland.

### Day 5: Lake Nakuru to Masai Mara

Stay: Masai Mara camp or lodge

- Explore Lake Nakuru in the morning before departing for the Masai Mara.
- Travel approximately 230 km over about 5 to 6 hours.
- Arrive in the Masai Mara in the afternoon and begin your first game drive.
- Look out for zebras, giraffes, elephants, and the first signs of the abundant wildlife that makes the Mara famous.
- Suggested accommodation options include Sarova Mara Game Camp, PrideInn Mara Camp, or Aruba Mara Camp.

### Day 6: Full Day in Masai Mara

Stay: Masai Mara camp or lodge

- Enjoy a full day of exploration in the Masai Mara with game drive distance around 100 to 150 km.
- Search for lions, cheetahs, elephants, buffalo, giraffes, zebras, and other iconic wildlife.
- During migration season, witness the Great Migration across the plains.
- Picnic lunch in the reserve surrounded by the sights and sounds of the wild.
- Optional hot air balloon safari may be arranged.
- Suggested accommodation options include Sarova Mara Game Camp, PrideInn Mara Camp, or Aruba Mara Camp.

### Day 7: Masai Mara to Nairobi & Departure

Stay: End of tour

- Enjoy a final morning game drive in the Masai Mara.
- Travel back to Nairobi, approximately 270 km with a driving time of 5 to 6 hours.
- Pass through the Rift Valley one last time before arriving back in the city.
- Transfer for departure, marking the end of your Kenya safari.
