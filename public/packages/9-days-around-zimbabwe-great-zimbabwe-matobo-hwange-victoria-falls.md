## Itinerary

### Day 1: Harare International Airport to Cresta Lodge Harare

Stay: Cresta Lodge Harare

- Upon arrival at the airport, you will be collected and transferred to Cresta Lodge in Harare.
- A pre-departure meeting with your guide will be scheduled while you relax at the lodge.
- Cresta Lodge, set in tranquil surroundings on the outskirts of Harare, offers a calm start to the journey.
- The rest of the day is yours to enjoy at leisure.

### Day 2: Harare to Great Zimbabwe Ruins

Stay: Clevers Lake View Resort

- After an early breakfast, explore Harare and visit the Balancing Rocks before departing south.
- The Chiremba Balancing Rocks, situated just outside Harare, are one of Zimbabwe’s most striking natural formations.
- Continue on to Great Zimbabwe in Masvingo.
- Check in at Clevers Lake View Resort and enjoy the late afternoon at leisure with beautiful lake views.

### Day 3: Great Zimbabwe to Matobo National Park

Stay: Big Cave Camp

- Explore Great Zimbabwe, the medieval stone city in the southeastern hills of modern Zimbabwe.
- Learn about its role as the capital of the Kingdom of Zimbabwe and its remarkable stone architecture.
- Later in the morning, depart for Matobo National Park south of Bulawayo.
- Arrive in the Matobo area and enjoy the late afternoon surrounded by dramatic rock formations and scenic landscapes.

### Day 4: Matobo National Park

Stay: Big Cave Camp

- Spend the day exploring Matobo National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
- The park is known for granite boulders, wildlife, rhinos, ancient San rock art, and exceptional viewpoints.
- Visit some of the park’s most memorable sights and enjoy a full day of discovery.
- Return to camp after a rewarding day in one of Zimbabwe’s most unique landscapes.

### Day 5: Matobo National Park to Hwange National Park

Stay: Hwange Safari Lodge

- Travel from Matobo National Park to Hwange National Park in western Zimbabwe.
- Hwange is the country’s largest national park and is famous for its elephant herds and classic safari landscapes.
- The park is also home to lions, leopards, buffalo, rhinos, giraffes, zebras, wildebeest, and many antelope species.
- Settle in and prepare for the wildlife experiences ahead.

### Day 6: Hwange National Park

Stay: Hwange Safari Lodge

- Enjoy a full-day game drive in the main park complete with lunch and refreshments.
- Choose a preferred picnic site for the lunch break.
- Hwange Safari Lodge is located near the entrance to the park and often offers wildlife sightings near the waterhole.
- Spend the day immersed in one of southern Africa’s most celebrated wildlife destinations.

### Day 7: Hwange National Park to Victoria Falls

Stay: Victoria Falls lodge

- Continue the journey north to Victoria Falls, one of the world’s great natural wonders.
- Conclude the day with a relaxing sunset cruise on the Zambezi River.
- Watch for wildlife along the riverbanks as you glide through the late afternoon light.
- Arrive in Victoria Falls ready for the final stage of your Zimbabwe adventure.

### Day 8: Victoria Falls

Stay: Victoria Falls lodge

- Visit Victoria Falls in either the morning or afternoon.
- Choose between a guided tour or a self-guided walk through the rainforest paths and viewpoints.
- Explore the cool, mist-filled forest environment and enjoy unforgettable views of the falls.
- In the evening, enjoy your final dinner at the Boma dinner and drum show, one of the highlights of the tour.

### Day 9: Transfer to Victoria Falls Airport

Stay: End of tour

- Today marks the final day of the journey after traveling across Zimbabwe from Harare to Victoria Falls.
- There are no scheduled activities, but if time permits, an additional activity may be arranged before departure.
- Transfer to Victoria Falls Airport for your onward travel.
- Your guide will drop you off at the airport, marking the end of the tour.
