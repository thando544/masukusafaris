## Itinerary

### Day 1: Windhoek to Sossusvlei

Stay: Namib Desert Lodge

- Depart Windhoek and travel through scenic mountain passes into the Namib Desert.
- Arrive at Sossusvlei and settle into your lodge.
- Enjoy a late afternoon nature drive and relax at the lodge.

### Day 2: Sossusvlei & Dead Vlei

Stay: Namib Desert Lodge

- Visit Sossusvlei and Dead Vlei with stops at Dune 45.
- Climb dunes and enjoy unforgettable desert views.
- Explore Sesriem Canyon before heading to Swakopmund.

### Day 3: Swakopmund

Stay: Swakopmund Sands

- Enjoy a Living Desert tour exploring unique desert wildlife.
- Optional activities include quad biking, sandboarding, and scenic flights.
- Relax and explore the coastal town.

### Day 4: Swakopmund to Twyfelfontein

Stay: Damaraland Lodge

- Travel along the Skeleton Coast visiting Cape Cross seal colony.
- Continue into Damaraland past Brandberg Mountain.
- Explore dramatic landscapes and rock formations.

### Day 5: Twyfelfontein to Etosha National Park

Stay: Etosha Safari Lodge

- Enter Etosha National Park, one of Africa’s top wildlife reserves.
- Enjoy first game drive spotting elephants, lions, and rhinos.

### Day 6: Etosha National Park

Stay: Etosha Safari Lodge

- Full-day game drives exploring waterholes and wildlife.
- Relax at the lodge between drives.

### Day 7: Etosha East

Stay: Mushara Lodge

- Drive across Etosha toward the eastern region.
- Experience diverse landscapes and wildlife sightings.

### Day 8: Mahango Game Reserve

Stay: Okavango River Lodge

- Travel to the Caprivi Strip.
- Enjoy sunset views along the Okavango River.
- Spot elephants, hippos, and buffalo.

### Day 9: Mahango & Bwabwata

Stay: Okavango River Lodge

- Game drives and optional boat cruise on the Okavango River.
- Excellent wildlife viewing including predators and birdlife.

### Day 10: Bwabwata National Park

Stay: Kwando River Lodge

- Explore Kwando River channels by boat.
- Search for elephants, lions, wild dogs, and more.

### Day 11: Chobe River (Botswana)

Stay: Chobe Safari Lodge

- Cross into Botswana via Ngoma Gate.
- Enjoy views over the Chobe River and surrounding wildlife.

### Day 12: Chobe National Park

Stay: Chobe Safari Lodge

- Full-day safari with game drive and boat cruise.
- Witness large elephant herds and diverse wildlife.

### Day 13: Victoria Falls

Stay: A' Zambezi River Lodge

- Transfer to Victoria Falls.
- Explore the falls and enjoy optional activities.
- End of unforgettable multi-country safari.
