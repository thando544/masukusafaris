## Itinerary

### Day 1: Arrival in Livingstone

Stay: Livingstone lodge

- Fly to Livingstone, where you will be met on arrival by the Africa Zim Travel representative.
- Transfer to your lodge with luggage assistance from the driver guide.
- Spend the remainder of the day on a sunset cruise on the Zambezi River.
- Receive a briefing about your tour itinerary.

### Day 2: Guided Falls Tour Zambia Side

Stay: Livingstone lodge

- After breakfast, enjoy a walking tour of Victoria Falls from the Zambia side.
- Experience one of the Seven Natural Wonders of the World.
- Spend the rest of the day at leisure.
- Optional activities may include a helicopter flight over the falls, a lion walk, and a visit to the Zimbabwe side.

### Day 3: Chobe National Park

Stay: Chobe area / lodge

- Travel from Livingstone to Chobe National Park in Botswana, approximately 100 km west of Victoria Falls.
- Explore one of Africa’s finest wildlife destinations, home to around 450 bird species.
- Experience game drives in 4x4 open safari vehicles.
- Enjoy a boat cruise on the Chobe River.
- Look out for the region’s famous large elephant population.

### Day 4: Chobe Morning Drive

Stay: Chobe area / lodge

- Continue exploring Chobe National Park through game drives and boat cruises on the Chobe River.
- Stay near Chobe Safari Lodge in Kasane, on the banks of the Chobe River.
- Experience the unique setting where Botswana, Namibia, Zimbabwe, and Zambia meet.
- Enjoy more wildlife viewing in one of southern Africa’s most celebrated safari regions.

### Day 5: Airport Transfer

Stay: End of tour

- Transfer to Livingstone Airport or Victoria Falls International Airport for your departure.
