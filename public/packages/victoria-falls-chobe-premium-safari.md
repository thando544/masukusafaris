## Itinerary

### Day 1: Pick-up from Victoria Falls Airport and Sunset Cruise

Stay: Shongwe Lookout Lodge or Victoria Falls Pioneers Lodge

- Arrival at Victoria Falls International Airport and transfer to accommodation.
- Afternoon pick-up for a sunset cruise on the Zambezi River.
- Enjoy finger foods, an open bar, birdlife, and wildlife along the riverbanks.
- End the day with a breathtaking Zimbabwean sunset.

### Day 2: Guided Tour of the Falls from Both Sides and Livingstone City Tour

Stay: Shongwe Lookout Lodge or Victoria Falls Pioneers Lodge

- Breakfast at the lodge.
- Guided tour of Victoria Falls from both the Zimbabwean and Zambian sides.
- Continue to Livingstone for a cultural and historical city tour.
- Visit the Livingstone Museum and local markets.
- In the evening enjoy a night game drive at Nakavango Private Game Reserve.
- End with refreshments, snacks, and a 3-course barbecue feast under the stars.

### Day 3: Full Day Chobe Trip, Botswana from Victoria Falls

Stay: Shongwe Lookout Lodge or Victoria Falls Pioneers Lodge

- Transfer from Victoria Falls to the Kazungula border.
- Meet your guide and enjoy either a Chobe River cruise or game drive first.
- Morning tea, coffee, and biscuits served during the experience.
- Lunch included before the afternoon safari activity.
- Return to Victoria Falls around late afternoon.

### Day 4: Airport Drop-off

Stay: End of tour

- Transfer back to Victoria Falls International Airport.
- Pick-up is scheduled according to your departure flight details.
