## Itinerary

### Day 1: Arrival in Victoria Falls, Zimbabwe – Transfer & Overnight Stay

Stay: Victoria Falls hotel

- Arrive in Victoria Falls and enjoy a comfortable transfer to your hotel.
- Settle in, refresh, and prepare for your safari experience.
- Take in the atmosphere of one of Africa’s most iconic destinations.
- Overnight stay in Victoria Falls.

### Day 2: Guided Tour of Victoria Falls

Stay: Victoria Falls hotel

- Embark on an exhilarating guided tour of Victoria Falls.
- Take in breathtaking vistas, thundering waters, and the misty spray that gives the falls the name 'The Smoke That Thunders'.
- Capture stunning photographs and enjoy the unforgettable atmosphere of this natural wonder.
- Return to your hotel for overnight stay.

### Day 3: Victoria Falls to Chobe – Afternoon Boat Cruise

Stay: Kasane / Chobe lodge

- Depart Victoria Falls and travel to Kasane in the Chobe region.
- Arrive in the afternoon in time for a boat cruise on the Chobe River.
- Enjoy wildlife sightings and beautiful river scenery as the day winds down.
- Settle into your lodge for dinner and overnight stay.

### Day 4: Full-Day Safari in Chobe National Park

Stay: Kasane / Chobe lodge

- Rise early for a full-day safari adventure in Chobe National Park.
- Explore diverse habitats from floodplains to woodlands in search of lions, leopards, elephants, and more.
- Stop for a picnic lunch in the bush before continuing your wildlife tracking through the afternoon.
- Return to your lodge in the evening for dinner and overnight stay.

### Day 5: Chobe to Elephant Sands

Stay: Elephant Sands Lodge

- Drive from Chobe to Elephant Sands.
- Enjoy a wildlife-focused safari lodge experience where elephants are often the stars of the show.
- Relax and watch elephants up close in their natural environment.
- Spend the evening enjoying the classic African bush atmosphere.

### Day 6: Elephant Sands to Gweta

Stay: Gweta lodge

- After breakfast at Elephant Sands Lodge, depart for a short and scenic drive to Gweta.
- Travel through open stretches of Botswana’s semi-arid landscape with acacia trees and wide horizons.
- Enjoy the comfort of the journey and occasional wildlife sightings along the way.
- Arrive in Gweta, the gateway to the magnificent Makgadikgadi Pans.

### Day 7: Gweta to Maun via the Makgadikgadi Pans

Stay: Maun lodge

- Depart Gweta before sunrise for a memorable drive toward the vast and shimmering Makgadikgadi Pans.
- Enjoy a picnic breakfast on the pans with fresh coffee, tea, pastries, fruit, and light breakfast snacks.
- Take photographs and soak in the quiet beauty of one of Africa’s most unique landscapes.
- Continue south toward Maun through open savannah, rural villages, and scattered baobab trees.
- Arrive in Maun in the afternoon and relax at your lodge.

### Day 8: Okavango Delta Excursion

Stay: Maun lodge

- Depart from your lodge in Maun in a comfortable 4x4 safari vehicle with an experienced guide.
- Travel through semi-arid savannah, scattered villages, and palm-lined waterways toward the Delta.
- Look out for wildlife such as giraffes, zebras, and elephants in the open plains.
- After the day’s excursion, return to Maun.

### Day 9: Scenic Flight Over the Okavango Delta

Stay: End of tour

- Enjoy a scenic flight over the Okavango Delta, one of the most breathtaking ways to experience this UNESCO World Heritage Site.
- Take in a bird’s-eye view of winding waterways, islands, and wildlife from above.
- Flights usually depart from Maun when the light is soft and wildlife activity is at its best.
- Board a light aircraft with large windows for unobstructed views and unforgettable photography opportunities.
- This scenic flight provides a perfect finale to your safari.
